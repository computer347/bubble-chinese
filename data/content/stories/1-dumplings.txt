今天是一月一日。 | Today is the first of January.
我们家人都在家，我们做饺子。 | The whole family is at home, and we're making dumplings.
爸爸和哥哥做饺子，妈妈做菜。 | Dad and my brother make the dumplings, and Mum cooks the other dishes.
我和妹妹也想做饺子。 | My sister and I want to make dumplings too.

我的饺子很大，妹妹的饺子很小。 | My dumplings are big, and my sister's are tiny.
哥哥的饺子非常漂亮。 | My brother's dumplings are beautiful.
爸爸说：大的小的都好吃！ | Dad says: "Big or small, they're all tasty!"

晚上六点，我们吃饺子。 | At six in the evening, we eat the dumplings.
我们一个一个吃。 | We eat them one by one.
妹妹问：这个饺子里有什么？ | My sister asks: "What's inside this dumpling?"
饺子里有一块钱！ | There's a one-kuai coin inside!
爸爸说：你明年有很多钱！ | Dad says: "You'll have lots of money next year!"
妹妹非常高兴。 | My sister is thrilled.
她说：我明年想买很多苹果。 | She says: "Next year I want to buy lots of apples."
