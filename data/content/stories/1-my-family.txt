我叫{小文=Xiǎo Wén}，今年十岁。 | My name is Xiaowen, and I'm ten this year.
我家有五个人。 | There are five people in my family.
爸爸，妈妈，哥哥，妹妹和我。 | Dad, Mum, my big brother, my little sister and me.
爸爸妈妈都是老师。 | Mum and Dad are both teachers.

我哥哥今年十六岁。 | My brother is sixteen this year.
他有女朋友，她叫{丽丽=Lìli}。 | He has a girlfriend called Lili.
我妹妹很小，她四岁。 | My little sister is very small; she's four.

我们家有一只狗，没有猫。 | We have a dog, but no cat.
狗的名字叫{大黑=Dà Hēi}。 | The dog is called Dahei.
{大黑=Dà Hēi}是我的好朋友。 | Dahei is my good friend.
它也是哥哥和妹妹的好朋友。 | It's my brother's and sister's good friend too.
妹妹很爱{大黑=Dà Hēi}，{大黑=Dà Hēi}也很爱她。 | My sister loves Dahei, and Dahei loves her too.
我们都是{大黑=Dà Hēi}的家人。 | We're all Dahei's family.

你家有狗吗？ | Do you have a dog?
