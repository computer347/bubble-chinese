你好！我叫{王丽=Wáng Lì}。 | Hello! My name is Wang Li.
我是中国人，今年十八岁。 | I'm Chinese, and I'm eighteen this year.
我是学生。 | I'm a student.
这是我的朋友{小月=Xiǎo Yuè}。 | This is my friend Xiaoyue.
她也是学生，我们是同学。 | She's a student too; we're classmates.
我们的老师是中国人。 | Our teacher is Chinese.

那是谁？ | Who's that?
他不是中国人，也不是我们的同学。 | He isn't Chinese, and he isn't one of our classmates.
{小月=Xiǎo Yuè}不认识他，我也不认识他。 | Xiaoyue doesn't know him, and neither do I.
他是学生吗？他是哪国人？ | Is he a student? Where is he from?
他今年几岁？他叫什么名字？ | How old is he? What's his name?

你们好！我叫{大卫=Dàwèi}。 | "Hello, everyone! My name is David."
我是你们的老师。 | "I'm your teacher."
老师好！ | "Hello, teacher!"
我们很高兴认识您。 | "We're very glad to meet you."
我也很高兴认识你们！ | "I'm very glad to meet you too!"
