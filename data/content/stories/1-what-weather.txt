今天是星期六。 | Today is Saturday.
早上七点，天气很冷。 | At seven in the morning, it's very cold.
我穿很多衣服。 | I put on lots of clothes.

中午十二点，天气很热。 | At twelve noon, it's very hot.
我的衣服太多了！ | I'm wearing far too many clothes!
我喝很多水。 | I drink lots of water.

下午三点半，下雨了。 | At half past three in the afternoon, it starts to rain.
雨很大，我没有车，也没有出租车。 | The rain is heavy, and I've no car and no taxi.
我的衣服都是雨水了。 | My clothes are soaked through.

晚上，天真冷，下雪了！ | In the evening it's really cold, and it snows!
这是什么天气？ | What weather is this?
一天有冷，有热，有雨，还有雪。 | In one day there's cold, heat, rain, and snow as well.

明天天气怎么样？ | What will the weather be like tomorrow?
明天我穿什么衣服？ | What should I wear tomorrow?
我想了想。 | I think it over.
明天是星期天，我在家，哪儿也不去！ | Tomorrow is Sunday. I'm staying home and going nowhere!
