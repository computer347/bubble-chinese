unit hello | Hello | 你好 | Daily life: interaction, polite behaviour (交往)
note Chinese has two words for "you": 你 nǐ, for friends and people your own age, and 您 nín, a polite "you" for elders, teachers and people you want to show respect to. 您好 is a respectful hello.
note In class, students greet their teacher together with 老师好! ("hello, teacher"). Among friends a greeting is often simply the other person's name.

lesson hello-1 | Hello
new 你好 您 好 你 我 很 吗 呢 也 再见
say A | 你好 ！ | Hello!
say B | 您 好 ！ | Hello! (polite)
say A | 你 好 吗 ？ | How are you?
say B | 我 很 好 。 你 呢 ？ | I'm fine. And you?
say A | 我 也 很 好 。 | I'm fine too.
say B | 再见 ！ | Goodbye!
say A | 再见 ！ | Goodbye!
build 我/S 很/A 好/V 。 | I'm fine.
build 我/S 也/A 很/A 好/V 。 | I'm fine too.
build 你/S 好/V 吗/X ？ | How are you?
build 你/S 呢/X ？ | And you?

lesson hello-2 | In class
new 大家 老师 学生 是 不 都 谢谢 不客气 对不起 没关系
say A | 大家 好 ！ | Hello, everyone!
say B | 老师 好 ！ | Hello, teacher!
say A | 大家 都 是 学生 吗 ？ | Is everyone here a student?
say B | 是 ， 我 是 学生 。 | Yes, I'm a student.
say C | 对不起 ， 我 不 是 学生 ， 我 是 老师 。 | Sorry, I'm not a student, I'm a teacher.
say A | 没关系 ！ 您 好 ！ | That's all right! Hello!
say B | 老师 ， 谢谢 ！ | Thank you, teacher!
say A | 不客气 。 | You're welcome.
build 我/S 是/V 学生/O 。 | I'm a student.
build 我/S 不/A 是/V 老师/O 。 | I'm not a teacher.
build 大家/S 都/A 是/V 学生/O 。 | Everyone is a student.
build 你/S 是/V 老师/O 吗/X ？ | Are you a teacher?

unit me | About me | 我 | Basic information: personal information (个人信息)
note In a Chinese name the family name comes first. In 王丽 Wáng Lì, 王 is the family name and 丽 the given name. Most family names are a single character.
note People are often addressed by family name and title, like 李老师 ("Teacher Li"), rather than by their given name.
note 你几岁？ expects a small number, so it is how you ask a child's age. With adults, 你多大？ is more usual, and elders are asked more politely still.

lesson me-1 | Names
new 叫 什么 名字 这 那 他 她 他们 我们 你们
say A | 你好 ！ 你 叫 什么 名字 ？ | Hello! What's your name?
say B | 我 叫 {大卫=Dàwèi} 。 你 呢 ？ | I'm David. And you?
say A | 我 叫 {王丽=Wáng Lì} 。 | I'm Wang Li.
say B | 这 是 你们 老师 吗 ？ | Is this your teacher?
say A | 是 ， 她 是 我们 老师 ， 她 叫 {李=Lǐ} 老师 。 | Yes, she's our teacher. She's Teacher Li.
say B | 那 是 {小明=Xiǎomíng} 吗 ？ | Is that Xiaoming?
say A | 不 是 。 他 叫 {小华=Xiǎohuá} 。 | No. He's called Xiaohua.
say B | 他们 都 是 学生 吗 ？ | Are they all students?
say A | 是 ， 他们 都 是 学生 。 | Yes, they're all students.
build 你/S 叫/V 什么+名字/O ？ | What's your name?
build 这/S 是/V 我们+老师/O 。 | This is our teacher.
build 他/S 不/A 是/V 老师/O 。 | He isn't a teacher.
build 他们/S 都/A 是/V 学生/O 。 | They're all students.

lesson me-2 | Where are you from?
new 认识 高兴 哪 国 人 中国 谁 的 朋友 同学

say B | 我 也 很 高兴 ！ 你 是 哪 国 人 ？ | Nice to meet you too! Where are you from?
say A | 我 是 {英国=Yīngguó} 人 。 你 是 中国 人 吗 ？ | I'm British. Are you Chinese?
say B | 是 ， 我 是 中国 人 。 | Yes, I'm Chinese.
say A | 那 是 谁 ？ | Who's that?
say B | 那 是 我 的 朋友 {小明=Xiǎomíng} 。 | That's my friend Xiaoming.
say A | 他 也 是 中国 人 吗 ？ | Is he Chinese too?
say B | 是 。 他 也 是 我 的 同学 。 | Yes. He's my classmate too.

build 那/S 是/V 谁/O ？ | Who's that?
build 他/S 是/V 我+的+朋友/O 。 | He's my friend.

unit numbers | Numbers and dates | 数字 | Basic information: event information (事件信息)
note Before a measure word, "two" is 两 liǎng, not 二 èr: 两个人, two people. 二 is for counting and for numbers like 十二.
note Dates run from big to small: year, month, day. Years are read digit by digit (二零二六年), and in speech the day of the month is 号.

lesson numbers-1 | Counting
new 一 二 三 四 五 六 七 九 两 个
say A | 你们 几 个 人 ？ | How many of you are there?
say B | 一 ， 二 ， 三 ， 四 。 我们 四 个 人 。 | One, two, three, four. There are four of us.
say A | 他们 呢 ？ | And them?
say B | 他们 两 个 人 。 | There are two of them.
say A | {小明=Xiǎomíng} 几 岁 ？ | How old is Xiaoming?
say B | 他 七 岁 。 他 的 朋友 九 岁 。 | He's seven. His friend is nine.
say A | 那 是 几 ？ | What number is that?
say B | 那 是 六 ， 不 是 九 ！ | That's six, not nine!
say A | 五 ， 六 ， 七 ， 八 。 | Five, six, seven, eight.
build 我们/S 四+个+人/V 。 | There are four of us.
build 你们/S 几+个+人/V ？ | How many of you are there?
build 他/S 七+岁/V 。 | He's seven.
build 那/S 是/V 六/O 。 | That's six.

lesson numbers-2 | Dates
new 今天 明天 昨天 月 号 星期 星期天 年 零 去年 明年
say A | 今天 几 月 几 号 ？ | What's the date today?
say B | 今天 九 月 二 十 七 号 。 | Today is the 27th of September.
say A | 今天 星期 几 ？ | What day is it today?
say B | 今天 星期 六 ， 明天 星期天 。 | Today is Saturday, tomorrow is Sunday.
say A | 昨天 是 星期 五 。 | Yesterday was Friday.
say B | 今年 是 二 零 二 六 年 。 | This year is 2026.
say A | 去年 是 二 零 二 五 年 ， 明年 是 二 零 二 七 年 。 | Last year was 2025, next year is 2027.
build 今天/S 星期+六/V 。 | Today is Saturday.
build 明天/S 是/V 星期天/O 。 | Tomorrow is Sunday.
build 今天/S 几+月+几+号/V ？ | What's the date today?
build 今年/S 是/V 二+零+二+六+年/O 。 | This year is 2026.

unit family | Family | 家人 | Basic information: personal information, family and friends (个人信息)
note Chinese names older and younger siblings differently: 哥哥 and 姐姐 are older brother and sister, 弟弟 and 妹妹 younger ones.
note 个 is the everyday measure word, but animals take 只: 一只猫, a cat.

lesson family-1 | My family
new 家 有 爸爸 妈妈 哥哥 姐姐 弟弟 妹妹 和 没有
say A | 你 家 有 几 个 人 ？ | How many people are in your family?
say B | 我 家 有 五 个 人 。 | There are five of us.
say B | 爸爸 ， 妈妈 ， 哥哥 ， 妹妹 和 我 。 | Dad, Mum, my older brother, my younger sister and me.
say A | 你 有 姐姐 吗 ？ | Do you have an older sister?
say B | 我 没有 姐姐 。 你 呢 ？ | I don't have an older sister. And you?
say A | 我 有 一 个 弟弟 ， 没有 哥哥 。 | I have a younger brother, and no older brother.
say B | 你 的 弟弟 几 岁 ？ | How old is your younger brother?
say A | 他 九 岁 。 | He's nine.
build 我+家/S 有/V 五+个+人/O 。 | There are five people in my family.
build 我/S 没有/V 姐姐/O 。 | I don't have an older sister.
build 你/S 有/V 弟弟/O 吗/X ？ | Do you have a younger brother?
build 我/S 有/V 一+个+妹妹/O 。 | I have a younger sister.

lesson family-2 | Sons, daughters and pets
new 家人 儿子 女儿 男朋友 女朋友 爱 猫 只 狗 它
say A | 这 是 你 的 家人 吗 ？ | Is this your family?
say B | 是 。 这 是 我 的 儿子 ， 那 是 我 的 女儿 。 | Yes. This is my son, and that's my daughter.
say A | 你 的 儿子 有 女朋友 吗 ？ | Does your son have a girlfriend?
say B | 有 ， 他 很 爱 她 。 | He does, he loves her very much.
say A | 你 的 女儿 呢 ？ 她 有 男朋友 吗 ？ | And your daughter? Does she have a boyfriend?
say B | 她 八 岁 ！ 她 爱 我们 家 的 猫 。 | She's eight! She loves our cat.
say A | 我 家 有 一 只 狗 ， 它 叫 {大黑=Dà Hēi} 。 | We have a dog. It's called Dahei.
build 这/S 是/V 我+的+儿子/O 。 | This is my son.
build 他/S 很/A 爱/V 她/O 。 | He loves her very much.
build 我+家/S 有/V 一+只+狗/O 。 | We have a dog.
build 她/S 爱/V 我们+家+的+猫/O 。 | She loves our cat.

unit food | Eating | 吃饭 | Daily life: food and drink (饮食)
note Rice (米饭) is the everyday staple in the south of China; noodles (面条儿) and steamed buns (包子) more so in the north.
note The 儿 at the end of 面条儿 is erhua, typical of Beijing speech; many speakers simply say 面条.

lesson food-1 | What would you like?
new 吃 喝 想 要 饭 米饭 面条儿 茶 水 牛奶
say A | 你 想 吃 什么 ？ | What would you like to eat?
say B | 我 想 吃 米饭 。 你 呢 ？ | I'd like rice. And you?
say A | 我 要 面条儿 。 | I'll have noodles.
say B | 你 想 喝 什么 ？ 茶 吗 ？ | What would you like to drink? Tea?
say A | 不 ， 我 不 喝 茶 。 我 要 水 。 | No, I don't drink tea. I'll have water.
say B | 我 喝 牛奶 。 我 很 想 吃 饭 ！ | I'll have milk. I'm really hungry!
build 我/S 想/A 吃/V 米饭/O 。 | I'd like to eat rice.
build 你/S 想/A 喝/V 什么/O ？ | What would you like to drink?
build 我/S 不/A 喝/V 茶/O 。 | I don't drink tea.
build 我/S 要/V 面条儿/O 。 | I'll have noodles.

lesson food-2 | Meals
new 早饭 午饭 晚饭 包子 鸡蛋 面包 好吃 菜 水果 苹果
say A | 你 早饭 吃 什么 ？ | What do you have for breakfast?
say B | 我 吃 鸡蛋 和 面包 ， 喝 牛奶 。 | I have eggs and bread, and drink milk.
say A | 我 早饭 吃 包子 。 包子 很 好吃 ！ | I have steamed buns. They're delicious!
say B | 你 午饭 吃 什么 ？ | And for lunch?
say A | 米饭 和 菜 。 晚饭 我 吃 面条儿 。 | Rice and vegetables. For dinner I have noodles.
say B | 你 吃 水果 吗 ？ | Do you eat fruit?
say A | 吃 ， 我 很 爱 吃 苹果 。 | Yes, I love apples.
build 我/S 早饭/T 吃/V 包子/O 。 | I have steamed buns for breakfast.
build 包子/S 很/A 好吃/V 。 | Steamed buns are delicious.
build 你/S 吃/V 水果/O 吗/X ？ | Do you eat fruit?
build 我/S 晚饭/T 吃/V 面条儿/O 。 | I have noodles for dinner.
unit shopping | Shopping | 买东西 | Daily life: shopping (购物)
note Prices are in 元 yuán on labels, but in speech people say 块 kuài: 五块 is five yuan.
note 太…了 means "too…": 太贵了, too expensive. The 了 marks it as a reaction to how things are.

lesson shopping-1 | How much?
new 买 卖 钱 多少 块 元 贵 便宜 太 了 还
say A | 你好 ！ 苹果 多少 钱 ？ | Hello! How much are the apples?
say B | 苹果 五 块 。 | The apples are five yuan.
say A | 太 贵 了 ！ | That's too expensive!
say B | 不 贵 ， 很 便宜 ！ 你 买 几 个 ？ | It's not expensive, it's cheap! How many would you like?
say A | 我 买 两 个 。 你 还 卖 什么 ？ | I'll take two. What else do you sell?
say B | 我 还 卖 牛奶 和 面包 。 | I also sell milk and bread.
say A | 牛奶 多少 钱 ？ | How much is the milk?
say B | 三 元 。 | Three yuan.
build 苹果/S 多少+钱/V ？ | How much are the apples?
build 这/S 太/A 贵/V 了/X 。 | That's too expensive.
build 我/S 买/V 两+个/O 。 | I'll take two.
build 我/S 还/A 卖/V 牛奶/O 。 | I also sell milk.

lesson shopping-2 | Clothes and shops
new 衣服 件 穿 喜欢 好看 漂亮 这个 那个 商店 东西 超市

say B | 超市 的 东西 很 便宜 。 | Things at the supermarket are cheap.
say A | 我 也 喜欢 超市 ！ | I like the supermarket too!

build 你/S 喜欢/V 那个/O 吗/X ？ | Do you like that one?

unit travel | Getting around | 去哪儿 | Daily life: transport (交通出行)
note To ask a stranger anything, start with 请问, "may I ask": it is the polite way in.
note 坐 means "to sit", and it is also how you take a vehicle: 坐出租车, 坐火车, 坐飞机.

lesson travel-1 | Where are you going?
new 去 回 在 哪儿 哪里 这儿 那儿 来 到
say A | 你 去 哪儿 ？ | Where are you going?
say B | 我 去 超市 。 你 呢 ？ | To the supermarket. And you?
say A | 我 回 家 。 | I'm going home.
say B | 你 家 在 哪里 ？ | Where do you live?
say A | 我 家 在 那儿 。 | My home is over there.
say B | {小明=Xiǎomíng} 在 这儿 吗 ？ | Is Xiaoming here?
say A | 他 不 在 。 他 明天 来 。 | He isn't. He's coming tomorrow.
say B | 他 明天 到 ？ | He arrives tomorrow?
say A | 是 ， 明天 到 。 | Yes, tomorrow.
build 你/S 去/V 哪儿/O ？ | Where are you going?
build 我/S 回/V 家/O 。 | I'm going home.
build 我+家/S 在/V 那儿/O 。 | My home is over there.
build 他/S 明天/T 来/V 。 | He's coming tomorrow.

lesson travel-2 | Taxi, train and plane
new 怎么 坐 火车 飞机 开车 出租车 请问 车 请 可以
say A | 明天 你 怎么 去 {上海=Shànghǎi} ？ | How are you getting to Shanghai tomorrow?
say B | 我 坐 火车 去 。 你 呢 ？ | By train. And you?
say A | 我 坐 飞机 。 | I'm flying.
say B | 你 爸爸 开车 吗 ？ | Does your dad drive?
say A | 他 不 开车 。 我们 坐 出租车 。 | He doesn't drive. We take taxis.
say A | 请问 ， 这 是 出租车 吗 ？ | Excuse me, is this a taxi?
say C | 是 ， 这 是 我 的 车 。 请 坐 ！ | Yes, this is my car. Please, have a seat!
say A | 我们 去 超市 ， 可以 吗 ？ | We're going to the supermarket, is that all right?
say C | 可以 ！ | Of course!
build 我/S 坐/V 飞机/O 。 | I'm flying.
build 你/S 怎么/A 去/V ？ | How are you getting there?
build 他/S 不/A 开车/V 。 | He doesn't drive.
build 我们/S 可以/A 坐/V 出租车/O 。 | We can take a taxi.

unit weather | Weather and time | 天气 | Basic information: environment, weather (环境信息)
note 有点儿 means "a bit", and usually a bit too much of something unwelcome: 有点儿冷, a bit cold.
note Clock times: 点 is o'clock, 分 minutes and 半 half past: 九点半 is half past nine.

lesson weather-1 | The weather
new 天气 怎么样 冷 热 有点儿 天 真 下雨 雨 雪
say A | 今天 天气 怎么样 ？ | What's the weather like today?
say B | 有点儿 冷 。 这 几 天 真 热 ！ | A bit cold. It's been really hot these last few days!
say A | 明天 呢 ？ 明天 下雨 吗 ？ | And tomorrow? Will it rain?
say B | 明天 下雨 。 | It'll rain tomorrow.
say A | 我 不 喜欢 雨 。 我 喜欢 雪 ！ | I don't like rain. I like snow!
say B | 我 也 喜欢 雪 ， 雪 很 漂亮 。 | I like snow too, it's beautiful.
build 今天/T 天气/S 怎么样/V ？ | What's the weather like today?
build 今天/S 有点儿/A 冷/V 。 | It's a bit cold today.
build 明天/T 下雨/V 。 | It'll rain tomorrow.
build 我/S 喜欢/V 雪/O 。 | I like snow.

lesson weather-2 | What time is it?
new 现在 点 半 早上 分 早 上午 中午 下午 晚上
say A | 现在 几 点 ？ | What time is it now?
say B | 九 点 半 。 | Half past nine.
say A | 你 早上 几 点 吃 早饭 ？ | What time do you have breakfast?
say B | 七 点 。 | At seven.
say A | 你 真 早 ！ 我 八 点 十 分 吃 。 | You're early! I eat at ten past eight.
say B | 你 上午 在 家 吗 ？ | Are you at home in the morning?
say A | 上午 在 家 ， 中午 和 下午 不 在 。 | In the morning, yes; at noon and in the afternoon, no.
say B | 晚上 呢 ？ | And in the evening?
say A | 晚上 在 家 。 | In the evening I'm at home.
build 现在/S 九+点+半/V 。 | It's half past nine.
build 我/S 早上+七+点/T 吃/V 早饭/O 。 | I have breakfast at seven.
build 你/S 上午/T 在/V 家/O 吗/X ？ | Are you at home in the morning?

unit school | School | 学校 | Education: study and campus life (学习情况, 校园生活)
note 汉语 and 中文 both mean Chinese. 汉语 is the usual word for the language you study; 中文 is common for written Chinese, as in 中文书, a Chinese book.
note Lessons begin with 上课 and end with 下课. Starting and finishing work are 上班 and 下班: 上 for going to, 下 for leaving.

unit health | Feeling ill | 看病 | Daily life: health (医疗健康)
note 没事 means "it's nothing": 我没事, I'm fine. It is one of the most common reassurances in Chinese.
note When someone is ill, the usual advice is to drink plenty of water and rest: 多喝水, 多休息.

lesson health-1 | At the doctor's
new 生病 医院 看病 病 没事 休息 不要 睡觉 一点儿 睡
say A | 你 怎么 了 ？ | What's wrong?
say B | 我 生病 了 。 | I'm ill.
say A | 你 要 去 医院 看病 。 | You should go to the hospital and see a doctor.
say C | 没事 ， 不 是 大 病 。 | It's nothing, not a serious illness.
say C | 你 要 多 休息 ， 多 喝 水 。 | You need to rest and drink plenty of water.
say B | 我 可以 上班 吗 ？ | Can I go to work?
say C | 不要 上班 ， 你 要 睡觉 。 | Don't go to work; you need to sleep.
say B | 我 吃 一点儿 东西 ， 可以 吗 ？ | Can I eat a little?
say C | 可以 。 早 一点儿 睡 。 | Yes. And go to bed a bit early.
build 我/S 生病/V 了/X 。 | I'm ill.
build 你/S 要/A 休息/V 。 | You need to rest.
build 不要/A 上班/V 。 | Don't go to work.
build 我/S 想/A 睡觉/V 。 | I want to sleep.

lesson health-2 | A long day
